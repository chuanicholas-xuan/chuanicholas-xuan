## Projects (Things I've built) 

Some projects are **closed source**. Their repos are showcases with a description, a live link and an APK download.

### 💰 FinTrack · [repo](https://github.com/chuanicholas-xuan/fintrack-app)

A personal finance app for Android and the web that keeps all your accounts, spending, budgets and savings goals in one place, synced across devices. You can scan receipts, split bills on trips and track several currencies.

`Expo` `React Native` `Supabase` `Claude API`

**[🌐 Open web app](https://fintrack-phi-eight.vercel.app)** · **[📱 Download APK](https://github.com/chuanicholas-xuan/fintrack-app/releases/latest)**

### ☀️ Sunwise · [repo](https://github.com/chuanicholas-xuan/sunwise)

A day planner that keeps your plans, habits and to-dos together on one Today timeline. It has colour-coded time slots, swipe-to-complete, repeats, checklists and reminders, plus sync between devices with a code

`Expo` `React Native` `GitHub Actions`

**[🌐 Open web app](https://sunwise-phi.vercel.app)** · **[📱 Download APK](https://github.com/chuanicholas-xuan/sunwise/releases/latest)**
